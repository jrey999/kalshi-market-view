How the filter works

Kalshi reports the last traded price every hour, even when that trade was a single tiny lot crossing a book nobody else was quoting into. Two independent checks decide what the heavy line trusts: volume ≥ 10 contracts in that hour makes a trade real regardless of spread, and spread ≤ 10¢ decides whether the resting quote midpoint is a decent stand-in while nothing is trading. Between real prints, the line carries the last trusted price forward rather than resetting to the noisy one.
